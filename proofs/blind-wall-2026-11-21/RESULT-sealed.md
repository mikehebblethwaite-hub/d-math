Pass mark: (c) end 0.7 or more AND 0.2 above both its own start and (a). (c) ends at 0.654: below 0.7, no rise from its start,
below the cold control. RESULT: FAIL. With the blind sealed-ball wall rule, the run did NOT find hex from scattered balls.
Why, from the numbers: sealed walls barely flatten under the blind rule (mean flatness 5D thousandths = 0.078 with the Henry,
5 = 0.005 without), so the balls stay round and jam where they land; the Henry's pull holds them in place rather than letting
them settle. Hot without the Henry ordered a little more (0.787) by settling, still no hex (3 with six neighbours).
Note: psi6 counts balls with only 2 neighbours, which can score high on their own; the start values (0.59-0.66) show it.
