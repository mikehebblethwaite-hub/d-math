# Blind wall test: does the raft's hex come from the run, or from what we put in? (21 Nov 2026, ~09:15 UK)

Mike 09:11: he told the dream (balls go hex) before the lab ran, so the lab may have been shaped to please him.
Honest list of what the raft runs PUT IN: the balls were set by hand in hex; the run kept one "how flat" number per ball
and the DRAWING made the hexagons (faces half way to each touching neighbour). Not put in: cold jams, the warmth window,
scattered balls jamming into clumps (against the dream).

## The fair test (written and stamped BEFORE any run)
1. The wall rule is written BLIND by a fresh helper that is not told the dream, the raft, or any expected pattern: only
   "two warm thin plastic balls touching, suction from below; how does their shared wall flatten, how close do their
   centres sit". It works in its own empty folder (~/blindwall) and may not read the lab, notes or proofs. Its files are
   copied here and hashed/stamped before they are run with many balls. Nobody edits its rule.
   (Weak point, said plainly: the helper is the same kind of bot and may carry a general notion that hex is common.)
2. Each wall is its own: every touching pair has its own flatness, driven by that pair's warmth, shell, suction across
   it and how hard they press. Centres sit at the helper's sitapart(flat) distance, so flattened balls can sit closer.
3. Balls start SCATTERED by the world's chance (never set in hex). 100 balls, 250 mm cone tray at 2 degrees, holed plate
   (10 mm apart, 0.5 mm), the Henry at full power, 120 C from second 6, 300 s.
4. Judged by NUMBERS, not the picture: for every ball, its touching neighbours (centres within 2% of sitapart) and the
   six-fold order psi6 = |mean of e^(6i angle)| over them (1 = perfect hex around it, random ~0.3-0.5).
   Reported: mean psi6 over balls with 2+ neighbours, balls with psi6 >= 0.9, balls with exactly 6 neighbours.
5. Controls, same chance seed where possible: (a) cold, Henry on; (b) 120 C, Henry off; (c) 120 C, Henry on (the dream).
## Pass mark, fixed now
- "The run finds hex" only if in (c) the mean psi6 at the end is 0.7 or more AND at least 0.2 above both its own start
  and control (a). Anything less: "the run did not find hex from scattered balls", said plainly, whatever the pictures look like.
