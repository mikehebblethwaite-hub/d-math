RESULT: FAIL for both, as stamped: the heat adds almost nothing over the cold shaken balls (sealed +0.011, vented +0.017).
WHAT IT FOUND: the hand on the rig is the big variable. Shaken, the sealed runs packed the scattered balls into hex, cold or
hot: 0.914 and 0.925, with 39 and 45 of 100 balls six round one (still, the best was 30). So in the lab, shaking orders the
pack; the heat flattens the walls of whatever pack there is. Packing hex and sealing into a raft are two different steps.
Said plainly: the two cold shaken runs (sealed 0.914, vented 0.740) are the same physics when cold (a vent does nothing cold)
with a different world's chance: single runs vary a great deal. The next test of this runs several of each.
