# candle_wax_wick_data.json

Given by Mike J Hebblethwaite, 21 Nov 2026 13:40 (made with Gemini, for "Delta Hex OS / Math D simulations"; the wax data he
had asked Gemini for). Seven waxes (melting ranges, how thick when liquid, how fast a wick draws them) and six wicks.
Used by lang/lab/runs/candlespace.delta. The melting ranges agree with the usual published figures (paraffin 46-68 C,
beeswax 62-65 C, soy 49-54 C); the qualitative words (low, medium, high) are turned into numbers in the run, said there.
