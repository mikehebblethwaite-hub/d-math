# Are we clean and real: the all-systems check

From time to time (Mike, 19 Nov 2026: "the efficiency of the whole system we look at from time to time, and do an are we
clean and real sanity all systems check"). Each check is done for real, on the real thing, and its findings go in
FAILS.md (with a system for each) or are fixed at once.

## Clean
- Nothing running that nobody asked for (old test displays, rooms, servers); nothing left in scratch places.
- No rebuildable bulk kept (old builds, recordings no one needs, copies of downloads).
- No dead code: every function used, every file still part of something; old ways removed, not left beside new ones.
- The repo, the site downloads and the handover all say the same version.

## Real
- Every number shown comes from where it says (the machine's watts, the clock, the air's cells), not made up.
- Every book balances (energy in the lab and its things, the air, the water); a book that does not is a fail.
- Every "done" in HISTORY, README and HANDOVER is true when tried now; every model's limits are said where it is used
  (first outside warmth, the draught taken from the openings, air faster than real time).
- Nothing pretends: a guess is said as a guess, sure 1 to 11.

## Efficient
- The lab's pictures a second, the air's step time, dc's build time and program size, the OS's boot time: measured
  against the last check, and the biggest cost looked at first.
- Data sent anywhere compressed (THOUGHTS.md for the next ways to try).

## Sane
- Delta builds itself three times to the same bytes; all tests pass; the OS boots and answers from outside; the lab opens
  on a big and a small screen.

Log: each check adds a line here: the date, what was found, what was fixed.
